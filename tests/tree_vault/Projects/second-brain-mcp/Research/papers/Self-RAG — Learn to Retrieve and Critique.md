# Self-RAG — Learn to Retrieve and Critique

## Relevance to Second Brain MCP
The model emits reflection tokens to decide whether to retrieve at all, and then
grades whether each passage is relevant and whether its own answer is supported.
Adaptive retrieval: do not search when the question does not need it.
