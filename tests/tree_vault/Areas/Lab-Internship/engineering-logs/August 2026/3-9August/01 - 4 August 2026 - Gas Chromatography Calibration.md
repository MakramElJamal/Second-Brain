# 01 - 4 August 2026 - Gas Chromatography Calibration

Calibrated the GC with three hydrogen standards (1%, 5%, 10% in argon). Peak area
was linear in concentration (R² = 0.998). The thermal conductivity detector needed
a 20 minute warm-up before the baseline settled. Retention time for H2 was 1.9 min.
