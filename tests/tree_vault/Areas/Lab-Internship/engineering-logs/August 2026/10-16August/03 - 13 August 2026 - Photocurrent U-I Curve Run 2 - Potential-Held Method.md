# 03 - 13 August 2026 - Photocurrent U-I Curve Run 2 - Potential-Held Method

Instead of sweeping, each potential was held for 60 s with light chopped on and
off. This removed the drift problem from run 1.

## Conclusions
The potential-held method gives a clean photocurrent of about 0.04 mA at 0.6 V vs
RHE and should replace the linear sweep for all further CuNi characterization.
