## Model
Sunlight enters a water box, a dichroic filter at 45 degrees splits the spectrum,
the PV band goes to side panels and visible light continues to the photoreactor.
Water absorbs a large share of the PV band before it reaches the dichroic filter.

## Open questions
Filter angle tolerance, water depth, and whether a Fresnel concentrator is needed.
