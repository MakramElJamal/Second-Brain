# 03 - 22 October 2026 - Dichroic Filter Angle

The dichroic filter is specified for 45 degrees incidence in air. Submerged in
water the refraction changes the effective angle to about 32 degrees, which shifts
the cut-off wavelength. The spec sheet must be re-read for the water case.
